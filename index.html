Baguette

Baguette is a digital studio led by Artur Paikin, specializing in building high-performance, accessible websites and applications. We also maintain significant open source projects used by developers worldwide.

Browse our selected work below and reach out at hello@unebaguette.com to discuss your project.

  • Uppy

    uppy.io

    Uppy is a sleek, modular file uploader that integrates seamlessly with any application. It’s fast, intuitive, and frees you to focus on more important tasks than building a file uploader. We designed and developed Uppy from zero to 30k GitHub stars and 1M monthly npm downloads. Uppy features a modern UI with file previews, progress tracking, dark theme, metadata and image editing, external cloud service integration, camera and audio recording and much more.

  • Transloadit

    transloadit.com

    Transloadit is a versatile API for developers to transcode videos, extract thumbnails, transcribe audio, generate waveforms, and much more. We designed and developed a new homepage showcasing Transloadit’s features, services, and integration simplicity, while enhancing documentation, accessibility, and the internal admin dashboard interface.

  • Serebro Nabora

    serebronabora.com

    For this annual international typography conference in Moscow, we created a responsive bilingual website with a flexible admin interface. The solution makes it simple to update the conference program, rearrange content blocks, and modify information as needed.

  • Brownfox

    brownfox.org

    Independent type foundry Brownfox offers a fresh perspective on Latin and Cyrillic typefaces. We developed a minimalist website featuring an innovative, flexible type tester that allows customers to experiment with different fonts. The streamlined purchasing experience includes intuitive license selection options, making typography accessible to designers at all levels.

  • Let’s bike it!

    letsbikeit.ru

    Let’s bike it! promotes cycling culture through bold advocacy, creative campaigns, and transformative events that have established cycling as a viable, visible urban transportation option. We designed a gorgeous, versatile website that effectively delivers both brief news updates and in-depth editorial pieces on global urban development.

  • Bike to Work

    bike2work.ru

    Website design for Bike to Work days in Russia. Started in Moscow, now spread throughout the country, with roughly 10,000 participants. The admin interface allows to add and customize page blocks like Instagram feeds or columns and buttons, and even add cities with their own subdomains.

  • Local Food

    locallocal.ru

    Local Food organizes food markets, sells a food box full of products supplied by the local farmers and offers a coworking space for the aspiring food entrepreneurs. On the website you can learn more about each of their projects, submit an application or order a food box.

  • Arsenicum

    arsenicum.co.uk

    Arsenicum is a world-renowned fashion brand that designs clothing and creates unique fashion shows with screaming elegance, smart essentricity and wild fantasies which became real on the catwalk. We designed a website for Arsenicum that helps transmit those experiences through the lens of digital media.

  • Polytechnic Museum

    polymus.ru

    Polytechnic Museum is one of the oldest museums in the world. We’ve helped build the frontend UI for the new website from the ground up in 2014–2015.

  • Made by Sloths

    We built an online store that sells stickers and illustrations by designers from all over the world, powered by Shopify.
